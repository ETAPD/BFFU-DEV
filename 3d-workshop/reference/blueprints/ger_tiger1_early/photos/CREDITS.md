`scripts/tankgen/specs/ger_tiger1_early.py`; never shipped (3d-workshop is export-ignored). Each photo keeps its own
licence; CC BY-SA photos must keep the attribution below if passed on and stay under CC BY-SA. Tiger 131 is a running
restoration; its track links on the nose and turret are field stowage. The drawing stays the dimensional reference.
