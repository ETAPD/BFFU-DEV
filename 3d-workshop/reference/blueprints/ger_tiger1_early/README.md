# ger_tiger1_early — drawing

Source: the four-view of Tiger 131 in the Military College of Science, School of Tank Technology report of January
1944, **public domain** (UK Crown copyright expired), on Wikimedia Commons:
[Tiger Side View Left.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tiger_Side_View_Left.png),
[Tiger Top View.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tiger_Top_View.png),
[Tiger Front View.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tiger_Front_View.png),
[Tiger Rear View.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tiger_Rear_View.png).

`src_*.png` are the originals; `side/top/front/rear.png` the views calibrated against the drawing's own dimension
lines and resampled to 175 px/m (scales and datums in the spec docstring); `overlay_*.png` the model's outlines
(red) over them. The same drawing is the base of `ger_tiger1_mid` and `ger_tiger1_late` (their READMEs say which
parts differ). `photos/` holds the free-licence photos checked (credits in `photos/CREDITS.md`).
