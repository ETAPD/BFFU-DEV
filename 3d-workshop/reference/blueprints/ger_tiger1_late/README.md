# ger_tiger1_late — drawing

Traced from the public-domain Tiger 131 four-view of `ger_tiger1_early` (School of Tank Technology, January 1944;
the calibrated `side/top/front/rear.png` live in `../ger_tiger1_early/`) for the parts the late Tiger shares with it
(hull, wheel stations, turret shell, mantlet, gun), which the photos in `photos/` confirm. No free drawing of a
late Tiger I exists; the late changes (steel wheels in two layers, monocular sight, loader's periscope, Pilze, cast
cupola, no Feifel filters or smoke dischargers) are set from the photos and the documented production changes (spec
docstring). `overlay_*.png` are the late model's outlines over that drawing.
