# Pz IV Ausf. H museum photos (reference only)

Surviving Ausf. H tanks, downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and scaled to 1280 px. They are modelling reference (next to the four-view drawing) for
`scripts/tankgen/specs/ger_pz4h.py` and are never shipped (3d-workshop is export-ignored). Each photo keeps its own
licence; CC BY / CC BY-SA photos must keep the attribution below if passed on, and CC BY-SA ones stay under CC BY-SA.
Restored museum vehicles differ in stowage and late modifications (the El Goloso tank is in post-war Spanish
service), so the four-view drawing stays the dimensional reference; these photos are for checking shapes the drawing
hides or only outlines.

Notes: `26`/`27` are the experimental hydrostatic-drive Pz IV (Ausf. H hull) at the US Army Ordnance Museum; its
running gear and rear differ from production tanks. Photos of the Overlord Museum tank (Colleville-sur-Mer) were
left out: they were shot through reflective glass.

## What the photos showed (Nov 2026 check against the model)

- Confirmed: stepped cupola, mantlet box with round collar and long barrel jacket, sloping front roof plate,
  turret-skirt side panels with two-leaf doors and bent front panels, hull Schürzen hanging outside a rail with the
  leading plate's top dropping at the bow (Lešany), hinged diamond-plate front mudguards sloping down, main muffler
  across the rear plate.
- Without skirts (Thun, Belgrade): the bare turret has a narrow front plate and long angled cheeks with vision
  ports; consistent with the station shell in `ger_pz4h.turret`, but the photos are not orthographic, so the
  hidden turret walls keep the top-view dimensions.
- Not resolved: none of the restored tanks carries the two round items the drawing shows on the front-right guard
  (unit stowage), and the drawing's diagonal line on the guards near the bow has no counterpart on these vehicles.
